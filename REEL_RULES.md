# Standard rules for every reel, Short and video (all pages)

- **Mobile format:** 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps. Keep text in the safe area: x 90–990, below y 220, above y 1500 (Facebook/YouTube buttons cover the bottom ~20% and right edge).
- **Length 15–90 s, aim 30–60 s.** Voiceover ~300–550 Sinhala letters (AI Studio reads ~9 letters/s). Use 6–10 scenes so no scene sits longer than ~8 s.
- **Closed captions:** burn word-for-word captions of everything spoken, synced to the voice (one short phrase at a time, 1–2 lines, ≤ ~22 letters per line, white bold text with dark outline on a translucent box, lower-middle safe area). Time them from silencedetect pauses. Also save a matching `.srt` next to the MP4. Check a contact strip that captions match the speech and are never clipped.
- **Short captions:** post text is max 3 short lines (< ~150 characters before hashtags): hook with one emoji, one key line, a share line; then 3–4 hashtags. YouTube titles ≤ 60 characters.
- **Ask to share:** every caption has a share ask ((වටේ: "🔁 යාළුවන්ටත් share කරන්න"; E Positive: "🔁 <specific person> කෙනෙක්ට share කරන්න"; Honey Bunny Kidz: "💛 Share with a parent who'd love this")) and the end card/last voice line says it too.
